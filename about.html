About Polymer Products

OUR HERITAGE

Decades of Prestressing & Polymer Engineering Expertise

Dynamic Prestress (I) Pvt. Ltd. is an established Indian enterprise with extensive experience in the field of prestressing technology and infrastructure solutions. Over decades of dedicated industry leadership, the company has played a vital role in the design, manufacturing, supply, and installation of prestressing materials, post-tensioning systems, and specialized infrastructure components for bridges, flyovers, metro networks, and railway corridors.

As a specialized part of the Dynamic group, Polymer Products focuses on the manufacturing and supply of Elastomeric Bearings and Seismic Pads for civil engineering and bridge applications. Its manufacturing facility at Nashik, Maharashtra is supported by experienced technical personnel, including Rubber Technologists, Polymer Chemists, and Structural Engineers, alongside comprehensive in-house quality-control and testing laboratories.

Bearing Configurations

Proposed Bearing Types & Technical Drawings

Standard configurations manufactured in accordance with IRC:83 (Part II), UIC 772-2R, and RDSO bridge designs.

Type A (Plain Elastomeric Pad)

Un-reinforced plain elastomer pad for light load spans & precast seating.

Type B (Standard Laminated Bearing)

Standard multi-layer steel reinforced elastomeric bearing for highway & railway bridges.

Type C (Outer Steel Plate Bearing)

Equipped with thick outer top/bottom steel anchor plates for fixing to structure.

Type F (PTFE Sliding Bearing)

Dimpled PTFE & stainless steel sliding plate for high horizontal translation.
