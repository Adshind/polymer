About Company

We Committed To Helping You Achieve Your Structural Goals

Polymer Products is an established name in the civil engineering industry, specialising in the manufacture and supply of Elastomeric Bearings and Seismic Pads. The company has extensive experience in bridge bearing applications and has developed comprehensive manufacturing and testing capabilities to serve major infrastructure requirements.

Our Elastomeric Bearings are manufactured at our dedicated facility in Nashik, Maharashtra, supported by experienced Rubber Technologists, Structural Engineers, Chemists, and supervisory personnel. Over the years, Polymer Products has supplied bearings for prestigious railway, highway, bridge, and flyover projects across India.

Sister Concern: Dynamic Prestress (I) Pvt. Ltd.

Operates as a specialized manufacturing division within the Dynamic group, with decades of industry reputation in prestressing technology and civil solutions.

Working Process

Comprehensive Manufacturing Process & Quality Control

From raw elastomer compounding (NR/CR) and shot-blasted steel plate preparation to precision hydraulic vulcanization and proof-load testing, every step follows documented standard operating procedures (SOP).

Step 01

Compounding & Prep

Raw elastomer compounding (NR/CR), precision steel grit blasting, and multi-coat adhesive application.

Step 02

Vulcanization

High-tonnage heated hydraulic pressing & precision molding ensuring homogeneous steel-rubber bonding.

Step 03

Testing & QA

Rigid shear modulus, compressive stiffness and proof-load testing strictly conforming to IRC:83 & RDSO.
