Detailed Manufacturing Process & Machinery

Our manufacturing process follows a systematic sequence from material preparation to final vulcanization and rigorous testing.

Step-by-Step Sequence

Systematic Manufacturing Process Flow

Each stage is carried out with defined Standard Operating Procedures (SOP) and stage-wise quality inspections to maintain consistency and zero-defect reliability.

Step 01
Raw Material Inspection & Testing

Incoming inspection of raw Natural Rubber (NR) / Chloroprene (CR), carbon blacks, zinc oxide, sulfur, vulcanizing agents, and mild steel plates (IS:2062). NABL test certification verification.

Step 02
Compounding & Two-Roll Mixing

Precision weighing of chemicals and masterbatch mixing on heavy duty Two-Roll Open Mixing Mills to achieve homogeneous dispersion, uniform rheology, and batch-to-batch consistency.

Step 03
Calendering & Pre-Form Sheeting

Conversion of mixed rubber compound into continuous sheets of exact specified thickness with zero porosity and controlled dimensional tolerances using multi-roll calenders.

Step 04
Steel Plate Shot-Blasting & Priming

Steel laminates are sheared, edges radiused (R ≥ 2mm), grit shot-blasted to Sa 2.5 finish to remove scale/rust, chemically degreased, and coated with Chemlok adhesive bonding agents.

Step 05
Assembly & Mould Loading

Systematic alternating stacking of primed steel laminates and un-vulcanized elastomer sheets inside heavy CNC-machined steel moulds with precise registration spacers.

Step 06
High-Pressure Hydraulic Vulcanization

Curing under high hydraulic clamping pressure (over 1000 kN) and controlled temperature (145°C - 160°C) for pre-programmed cure cycle times to ensure total molecular cross-linking and permanent bond.

Step 07
De-Moulding, Trimming & Finishing

Careful extraction of vulcanized bearings, flash trimming, edge smoothing, inspection of outer protective rubber layer (≥ 4mm side / 2.5mm top-bottom), and initial visual check.

Step 08
Marking & Lot Identification

Hot-embossing / stenciling of unique lot number, bearing type, dimensions, manufacturer code, and production date onto each bearing for permanent field traceability.

Step 09
QA/QC Proof Load & Lab Testing

Proof load compressive stiffness testing, shear modulus determination, dimension verification, witness inspection by client engineers, and generation of manufacturer test certificate (MTC).

List of Plant Machinery & In-House Facilities

Our Nashik facility is equipped with industrial-grade rubber processing machinery, hydraulic curing presses, and precision testing equipment.

Hydraulic Vulcanizing Press
High tonnage multi-daylight heated hydraulic presses with PLC digital temperature controls.
Two-Roll Rubber Mixing Mill

Plate Shearing & Shot Blasting
Steel plate guillotine shearing, edge radiusing grinders, and enclosed grit shot-blasting chamber.

Computerized compressive load test frame calibrated for up to 1.5x design load verification.
