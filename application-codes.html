ISO 37 - 2017(E)

Title: Rubber, Vulcanized or Thermoplastic - Determination of Tensile Stress-Strain Properties.

International test method governing dumbbell test pieces, tensile strength (≥ 17 MPa), elongation at break (≥ 400%), and modulus at 100% and 300% elongation.

ISO 1431-1 - 2024

Title: Rubber, Vulcanized - Resistance to Ozone Cracking - Static and Dynamic Strain Testing.

Prescribes the ozone chamber test parameters ensuring elastomeric bearings exhibit zero micro-cracking when exposed to high ozone concentrations over 72 hours under static strain.
