Material Specifications

Raw Materials Used in Manufacturing

Conforming strictly to IRC:83 (Part II), RDSO, UIC 772-2R, IS:2062, and international structural standards.

ELASTOMER POLYMER

1. Elastomeric Raw Polymer (NR / CR)

The elastomer used in the manufacture of Elastomeric Bearings is specified in project documentation as either Natural Rubber (NR) or Chloroprene Rubber (CR - Neoprene) as the raw polymer base:

Natural Rubber (NR):

High elasticity, superior low-temperature performance, high tensile strength, and excellent fatigue resistance under continuous dynamic cyclic loading.

Chloroprene Rubber (CR):

Enhanced resistance to ozone, atmospheric weathering, UV degradation, mineral oils, and harsh environmental chemical exposure.

Elastomer Property Limits
  • Hardness (Shore A)60 ± 5 IRHD
  • Min. Tensile Strength≥ 17.0 MPa
  • Min. Elongation at Break≥ 400%
  • Max Compression Set (24h/70°C)≤ 35%
  • Accelerated Ageing Drop≤ 15% Max Change
  • Ozone ResistanceNo Cracking (20% Strain)
REINFORCING LAMINATES

2. Mild Steel Laminates (IS: 2062 / IS: 1079)

Laminates of mild steel conforming to IS: 2062 (Grade E250) or IS: 1079 or equivalent international grade are used as internal reinforcement plates.

Mandatory Standard Compliance:

The yield stress of the steel material shall not be lesser than 250 MPa. The use of any other materials like fiberglass or similar fabric as laminates is strictly not permitted for bridge bearing codes (IRC:83 / RDSO).

Each steel laminate undergoes rigorous edge radiusing, grit shot-blasting, chemical degreasing, and application of Chemlok bonding primer for chemical adhesion to rubber exceeding 7 kN/m.

Precision cut & deburred IS:2062 steel laminates ready for vulcanization

SLIDING MEDIA

3. Polytetrafluoroethylene (PTFE) & Stainless Steel

For free-sliding and guided-sliding bearing types, 100% virgin PTFE sheets conforming to BS 5400 / EN 1337-2 are dimpled and lubricated with special silicone grease, sliding against austenitic stainless steel (grade 316 / 304) with mirror-finish surface roughness Ra ≤ 0.1 μm.

Serviceability Limit State (SLS)
Maintains full elastic recovery under maximum operational dead + live loads.
Ultimate Limit State (ULS)
Safety factors prevent structural crushing or laminate de-bonding under extreme ultimate actions.
